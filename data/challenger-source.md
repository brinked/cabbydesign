# Challenger Designs: source data for the cabinet designer

Researched 2026-11-10. Nothing in this file is estimated. Where the manufacturer does not publish a value it says "not published". Dealer-sourced values are labelled DEALER-SOURCED.

## Summary

- **Manufacturer**: Challenger Designs, 24785 US 6 East, Nappanee, IN 46550, (574) 773-8200. All-aluminum, powder-coated outdoor cabinetry, shipped fully assembled.
- **The official site is `challengerdesignsllc.com`, not `challengerdesigns.com`.** `challengerdesigns.com` resolves to an unrelated Adobe Portfolio host (TLS certificate is for `*.myportfolio.com`; plain HTTP redirects to `portfolio.adobe.com/missing`). `challengeroutdoor.com` is an unrelated Turkish hunting-apparel shop. The site is WordPress, not Shopify; there is no `/products.json` and the WooCommerce store API returns 404.
- **Primary sources**: the four 2026 catalogs on the manufacturer's Resources page, all marked "Effective August 1, 2026":
  - Outdoor Kitchen (Custom Kitchens modules), 26 pages: https://challengerdesignsllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Outdoor-Kitchen-CATALOG.pdf
  - Coastal, 12 pages: https://challengerdesignsllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Coastal-CATALOG.pdf
  - Canyon, 5 pages: https://challengerdesignsllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Canyon-CATALOG.pdf
  - Cascade, 4 pages: https://challengerdesignsllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Cascade-CATALOG.pdf
  - Quote request form (REV 2/26): https://challengerdesignsllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/022526-Challenger-Design-Quote-Request.pdf
  - Web pages: https://challengerdesignsllc.com/products/custom-kitchens/ , /products/coastal/ , /products/canyon/ , /products/cascade-collection/ , /resources/
- **Prices: the manufacturer publishes none.** The public catalogs carry the line "MSRP = .50 of LIST" but the price column is removed from every public copy checked (2022, 2024, 2025 and 2026 editions). The site tells buyers to use a dealer or the quote form. The only prices in this file are a handful of DEALER-SOURCED figures for pre-configured islands and Canyon inserts. No per-module price for the Custom Kitchens line was found anywhere.
- **Model count in the table below**: 311 cabinet/island models (258 Custom Kitchens modules, 20 Coastal, 29 Canyon, 4 Cascade) plus 104 countertop, panel, filler, leg, shelf and accessory items, 415 rows in total.
- **Sizing model**: Custom Kitchens modules are fixed catalog sizes in 3" width increments, built to order, with appliance cabinets (grill, sink, refrigerator, power burner, kamado) built to the specific appliance's cutout. Coastal and Cascade are fixed pre-designed islands ("Models and layouts are as is, no deviations or changes"). Canyon is a fixed list of built-in inserts for masonry islands.

## All models

One table. Cabinet and island models come first (311 rows), then countertops, panels, fillers, legs, shelves and accessories (104 rows).

How to read W / D / H for the Custom Kitchens rows: the catalog prints only the width next to each item. The SKU suffix encodes width, height and depth in that order (for example `ODB-243528` is 24" W x 35" H x 28" D). The catalog confirms this reading wherever it prints full dimensions: `OPO-397832` is printed as "39"w x 78"h x 32"d", `OGGBD-453532` as "45" w x 32" d", `OCGC-303530` as "30" (30" deep)", End Panel `EP-28D` as "35"H x 28"D", and the 12"-deep door bases carry the note "12" DEEP CABINETS". Where a dimension rests on the SKU code alone and looks unusual, the cell says so.

"List price: not published" means the manufacturer's public catalog and website show no price for that item.

## Rules and dimensions

Sources for this section unless noted: 2026 Outdoor Kitchen catalog (https://challengerdesignsllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Outdoor-Kitchen-CATALOG.pdf), 2026 Coastal, Canyon and Cascade catalogs, and https://challengerdesignsllc.com/products/custom-kitchens/ .

### Base cabinet conventions (Custom Kitchens)

- **Height 35"**, including the standard legs. Supporting figures from the catalog: End Panels are 35"H; the Base Filler Panel and the Finished Back Panel are 31"H; the Fluted Filler Post is "31.5"H for ADA Legs, 35"H for Standard 4" Adjustable Legs"; standard leg sets are "4"-6" Tall".
- **Depth 28"** standard. Other catalog depths: 12" (shallow door bases, end cabinets, all overheads), 30", 32", 34" and 36" (specific grill, kamado and Evo bases), 29.5" (lockers).
- **Width**: 3" increments. Door base 12" to 48" plus 54"; drawer/door base 15" to 48"; drawer banks 15" to 33"; sink base 18" to 48"; gas grill/side burner base 18" to 66"; 9" accessory bases.
- **Countertop height**: Challenger tops are 1.25" thick. The Coastal islands, which use the same construction, are listed at 36.25" overall height including the top. A finished height for Custom Kitchens is not printed as a single number; 35" + 1.25" is the arithmetic.
- **ADA**: ADA sink bases are 32" high (SKU `OSB-xx3228 ADA`) on ADA legs that adjust 1.5" to 2.5". ADA legs cannot take a toe kick.
- **Legs**: 304 stainless adjustable 4" legs standard (sets adjust 4" to 6"), in Stainless Steel, Black or Bronze.
- **Toe kick**: optional clip-on powder-coated aluminum toe kick `17TK-94W`, 94" long, cut to size at installation, clips onto the 4" adjustable legs. The website calls it an "8' Clip-On Toe Kick Kit". Toe-kick height and setback: not published. Toe-kick extensions `TKE-2/3/4` are 1" x 2", 3" or 4" x 94".
- **Finished sides and backs**: "All cabinets have finished back panels, and finished side panels (when specified) at no additional cost." So backs are always finished (islands are fine); sides must be specified. Exceptions printed in the catalog: Open Storage, overheads, blind corner overheads and lockers "come with finished sides" (lockers also back); Inside Corner Overhead has "SIDES NOT FINISHED"; a separate Finished Side Panel `FSP-2831` exists "to finish side of power burner base".
- **Interior**: catalog images show a black interior; "Premium Finish" is the option for an interior matching the exterior colour. The standard interior colour is not stated in words.
- **Door count rule**: 12" to 24" wide = 1 door (specify LH or RH); 27" to 48" (54" on door, peninsula bases) = 2 doors. Gas grill bases: 51" to 54" = 3 doors, 57" to 66" = 4 doors.
- **Drawer rule**: drawer/door bases have 1 top drawer (5.5"H) up to 33" wide and 2 drawers from 36" to 48".
- **One-piece islands**: the Outdoor Island Base (OIB) joins cabinets into one fully assembled unit, 24" to 96" long, on 4"H adjustable legs or 3"H casters (4 up to 71", 6 from 72" to 96"). It is 3.125" shallower than the cabinets (24.875" for 28" deep cabinets). On casters it adds 1 7/8" to cabinet height, on legs 1 9/16". It cannot be used with free-standing appliances such as a refrigerator, with lockers, or with the Open Shelf unit.
- **Towel bar handle** adds 4 1/4" to a cabinet's width and 3 3/4" to the overall run; not available on 3 and 4 drawer bases.
- **Shallow cabinets**: 12" deep cabinets must be secured to another cabinet or a wall. Lockers and the pizza oven cabinet must be attached to a wall.

### Wall (overhead) and tall cabinets

- Overheads: 12" deep; heights 18", 24", 30", 36"; widths 12" to 36" in 3" steps; 1 door to 24", 2 doors from 27". 18" high has no shelf, 24" and 30" have 1 adjustable shelf, 36" has 2.
- Blind Corner Overhead 36" wide and Inside Corner Overhead 24" wide, each in the four heights. The inside corner cannot be at the end of a run or beside an opening; a 3" filler is recommended for door clearance.
- Lockers: 78", 84" and 90" high, 29.5" deep, 18" to 30" wide (90" also 36"). The 90" high lockers at 30" and 36" wide have 4 doors.
- Mounting height above the counter for overheads: not published.

### Corners

- **Inside Corner Base `OICCB-403528`**: 40", angled (diagonal) front with one 13.75" wide door, fixed shelf. Cannot be at the end of a run or beside an opening. The angle of the front and the wall length it occupies on each side are not printed (see Uncertain list).
- **Blind Base `OBBC-483528`** (48", 1 door) and **Blind Base w/Rear Access `OBBCRA-483528`** (48"). A 3" filler panel is recommended for door clearance.
- **Corner Ceramic Grill Base `OICGC-403528`**: 40" angled corner for a medium, large or XL kamado.
- **Outside Angle Base `OAB-283528`**: "Installs in corner or between two cabinets". No dimensions or angle printed.
- **Filler Angle Kits**: `FAK-30D` (30 degrees) and `FAK-45D` (45 degrees). Each kit has 2 angle pieces and 2 finished back panels (5.1875" wide for 30 degrees, 9.3125" wide for 45 degrees). They do not provide countertop support.
- **Outside Corner Panel Kit `OCP-KIT`** (specify widths) and **End Panels** `EP-28D/30D/32D/36D` (1 3/8" wide, 35"H) to support the countertop beside an appliance opening or at a corner.
- A true 90 degree L-corner is made with the blind base; 45 degree and 30 degree turns are made with the filler angle kits or the angled corner bases.

### Appliance openings (refrigerators and similar)

- Free-standing appliance openings are created between cabinets with a Framed Finished Back Panel (`FBP`, specify width, 6" minimum, 48" maximum, 31"H; a vented version `VFBP` exists) and End Panels for countertop support.
- Built In Appliance Base `BIAB` in 30", 36", 39", 42", 48".
- Coastal refrigerator openings are fixed: 21.5"W x 33.625"H (for a 21" refrigerator) or 24.5"W x 33.625"H (for a 24" refrigerator). Casters are not available on refrigerator islands.
- "Each grill, refrigerator, or sink cabinet is built according to the manufacturer's specifications to ensure a perfect fit." The quote form asks for brand and model of the gas grill, ceramic grill, power burner, side burner and mini refrigerator.

### Grill and burner bases

- **Gas Grill / Side Burner Base `OGGB`**: 18" to 27" wide are side-burner bases; 30" to 66" are gas grill bases. 12" tall appliance skirt, vented in floor and back panel, does not accommodate an LP tank. Also offered 32" deep in 42", 48", 54", 60", 66".
- **Deep Cut `OGGBD`**: 36" to 51", custom appliance skirt 16" tall, for grills needing a deeper cutout. One 32" deep size (45").
- **Grill Base w/Door & Drawer `ODDGGB`**: 30" to 48", sub-floor 12" from top of cabinet, 2 drawers 7.2"H, 1 RH door.
- **Grill base with warming drawer `OGGWDB`**: 36", 42", 48"; bottom false panel takes a warming drawer up to 14"H cutout.
- **Evo**: `OEGB-363528` for the 25G, `OEGB-393534` for the 30G (needs a 1 1/2" front countertop overhang).
- **Memphis**: `OMCB-183528L` control-panel base, left hinge only, installs left of the grill base; control box not included.
- **Power Burner Base `OPBB`**: 24", 27", 30", 36"; covers must be factory installed.
- **LP tank storage** is separate: Pull Out Propane Base (21", 20 lb tank), Drop-In Propane Base (18", 21", 24"; 20 lb), Drop-In Full Door (18", 21", 24"; 20 or 30 lb). All vented.
- **How wide a base a given grill needs: not published.** There is no grill-brand or cutout-to-cabinet-width chart in the catalog. The cabinet is cut to the appliance, and the catalog repeats "Please Provide Appliance(s) Brand & Model". Column supports carry the note "May not work with all grill models".
- **Insulated jacket: not published.** No catalog or page says whether a grill's insulating jacket/liner is required or not required in these aluminum cabinets. Do not assume either way.
- **Coastal grill cutout (fixed)**: maximum 30.75" cutout width x 22.5" cutout depth x 13.25" cutout height including countertop, "accommodates most 32" or smaller grills". The 83" kamado+grill islands allow 28.5" cutout depth. The Large Edition `COGI-79.25-LE` "fits up to 42" grill"; its cutout dimensions are not printed. The 2025 new-items flyer gives its make-up as 15" door drawer, 48" grill base, 18" waste bin, and gives `COGI-88.25-GDDG` as 36" grill base, 15" door drawer, 36" grill base. Coastal gas models are set up for a 20 lb LP tank with a cutout under the grill.
- **Cascade grill cutout**: "will fit most 32" grills (max cut out of 30 3/4)".

### Kamado (ceramic) grill bases

- 24" wide = medium or large grill; 30" and 33" wide = large or XL grill. 28" deep for medium or large, 30" deep for large or XL.
- "Island is recommended. If unit is to remain stationary against a wall, the cabinet must be 28" Deep for a Medium Grill, 32" Deep for a Large Grill, and 36" Deep for an Extra Large Grill. (Kamado Joe II & III are exempt from these specifications due to Hinge Size & Grill Height)".
- Shelf height is set to the grill brand and model. Dedicated bases exist for Kamado Joe Classic III (`OCGDB-303530`) and Big Joe III (`OCGDB-303536`).
- Coastal kamado islands name Large and XL Big Green Egg, Kamado Joe Classic I and II, Primo Kamado and 300, and Large Grill Dome on the product page; the grill must be specified when ordering.

### Countertops

- Challenger sells two tops: **.090" powder-coated aluminum** (`ACT`) and **16 gauge food-grade stainless steel** (`SST`), both 1.25" thick.
- Stock depths: **13", 18", 29", 30", 33"**. **"ALL PRICING IS COST PER LINEAL INCH"**. The per-inch prices are not published.
- "Top overhang is 1" on all sides; unless against wall."
- Backsplash: 4"H x 1" thick, aluminum (`ABS-4`) or stainless (`SSBS-4`), per lineal inch.
- Bar top with risers: 16"D x 1.25" thick (`ABT-16`, `SSBT-16`). Corner supports 8"W x 8"D x 5"H; centre supports 12"W x 5"H, one for every 5' of bar top. Bar top overlaps the countertop by 9" and overhangs the edge by 7". Column bar supports come 35" and 40.25" high, 5" or 14" deep.
- Top colours: Brushed Stainless Steel, Copper Vein, Silver Vein, Speckled Walnut, Textured Black, White Vein.
- Customer-supplied tops (granite, concrete and so on) are supported: "Cabinets are ready for countertop surface of your choice". The quote form requires the thickness of a customer-supplied top. Coastal and Cascade give a 10% discount on base price without the countertop.
- Sinks: `SINK-15` (15"W x 15"D x 5.5"H overall) and `SINK-30` (31.25"W x 18"D x 9"H overall, needs at least a 39" sink base with a side-mounted faucet). ADA sink bases need a customer-supplied sink.
